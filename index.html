05 — CASE STUDY

Profolio: A Portfolio Template Marketplace

An in-depth look at how I built a portfolio platform showcasing ready-made website templates using Next.js, MongoDB, and Prisma to make template discovery simple, polished, and fast.

The Challenge

Finding a polished portfolio template is often only the first step. Users still need a simple way to explore different styles, understand what each template offers, and turn one into a personalized website.

The Approach

I built Profolio as a template marketplace where users can explore ready-made designs and build a portfolio around their needs. I developed the responsive interface with Next.js and React, while MongoDB, Prisma, and NextAuth handled data, authentication, and user flows.
